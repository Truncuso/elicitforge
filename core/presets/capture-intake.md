output_contract: Emits RESOLVED_TERM (with optional RELATED) for every fuzzy intake term the grill settles, and HANDOFF_SUGGESTION with its required QUESTION line for anything unresolved — deferred for each branch left deliberately shallow by the rapid playbook, research or user-judgment for walls that intake cannot close. ADR_CANDIDATE only if an intake answer genuinely passes the three-condition gate. No artifact is written by the engine; the closing summary block restates every signal for the caller to file.

# capture-intake

Declarative preset. Selects the `rapid` playbook from the engine's closed style
enum (../engine.md) — breadth-first, closed or short-answer questions,
recommended answers stated as defaults acceptable by silence, and the lowered
stopping bar: stop once no branch is entirely unexplored. Nothing here forks the
engine; the techniques, stop rule, and `unattended:` behavior are the engine's.
